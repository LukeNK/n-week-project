### Characteristics

One special thing about fungi is that they form branches that are called **hyphae**. This is why you cannot just "remove" food mold by scooping the top, because there is probably a whole network bellow the surface.

And when we talk about a fungus, we would usually think about a mushroom. In fact, a mushroom is a spore-producing structure.
